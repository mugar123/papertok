# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want PaperTok to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of experience, background, or identity. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation harassment-free for all.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Credit others' work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer, [@mugar123](https://github.com/mugar123), through GitHub. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or block contributors as needed.
